How the fives work

Count the exposed pips. Below five, one card covers the other face down. At five, turn it face up. Our seven shows five plus two; their two shows just two. The numeral beside each team gives a quick check.

Clear, without losing the game

Real scoring fives, a dealer chip, and stacks of won tricks. These are useful parts of the interface, not background decoration. Scores also have numerals; trump also has a name; your partner and the makers are explicit. Cards stay familiar. Guidance explains the current decision, with the bower rule one click away.
